# User Personas

## P.1 Persona Overview

We design for four user categories:
1. Dental practice staff (primary users of the dashboard)
2. Dental practice owners (business stakeholders)
3. Patients (recipients of AI communication)
4. HeyJarvis operators (internal team managing the platform)

---

## P.2 Practice Staff

### P.2.1 Sarah — Front Desk Coordinator

**Demographics:**
- Age: 32
- Experience: 8 years in dental administration
- Tech comfort: Moderate
- Daily workflow: Appointment scheduling, patient check-in, phone calls, insurance verification

**Goals:**
- Reduce time spent on follow-up calls
- Fill appointment gaps caused by cancellations/no-shows
- Keep the schedule full without being pushy
- Maintain good patient relationships

**Pain Points:**
- Juggling phones, patients, and paperwork simultaneously
- Patients who say "I'll call back" and never do
- End-of-day schedule with empty slots that could have been filled
- Calling patients who don't answer (wasting time on wrong numbers)
- Forgetting to follow up with patients who submitted lead forms

**How HeyJarvis helps:**
- Automated outreach handles the initial contact
- AI qualifies patients before they reach her
- Human handoff queue gives her only the patients who need human attention
- Dashboard shows exactly what needs action

**Quotes:**
- "I spend at least an hour a day just calling patients back."
- "The ones who need us most are often the hardest to reach."
- "I don't want to feel like I'm harassing patients."

---

### P.2.2 Dr. Chen — Practice Owner

**Demographics:**
- Age: 45
- Role: Owner/operator of 2-location dental practice
- Experience: 15 years practicing, 8 years owning
- Tech comfort: High (invested in practice management software)
- Concerned with: Revenue, patient retention, staff efficiency, compliance

**Goals:**
- Recover lost revenue from missed opportunities
- Keep patients in the practice long-term
- Reduce staff burnout from repetitive tasks
- Maintain HIPAA compliance
- Get data-driven insights into practice performance

**Pain Points:**
- Doesn't know how many patients "disappear" after inquiry
- Staff turnover makes consistent follow-up hard
- Unclear which marketing efforts actually bring in patients
- Worried about HIPAA violations with new tools
- Cancellations and no-shows directly impact revenue

**How HeyJarvis helps:**
- Complete picture of where revenue leaks occur
- Automated recapture that's compliant and professional
- Analytics showing ROI
- Confidence that communication is HIPAA-compliant
- Reduced staff workload

**Quotes:**
- "If I could recover even 10% of my lost appointments, that's significant."
- "I need to know this is safe before I trust it with patient data."
- "Show me the numbers — I need to see the ROI."

## P.3 Patients

### P.3.1 Maria — Busy Professional

**Demographics:**
- Age: 34
- Occupation: Marketing manager
- Insurance: Employer-provided PPO
- Dental situation: Needs Invisalign consultation, interested but not urgent
- Communication: Prefers SMS, responds during work breaks

**Goals:**
- Get dental care on her schedule
- Understand costs and insurance coverage
- Not feel pressured or harassed
- Convenient communication

**Pain Points:**
- Can't take calls during work
- Forgets to call back after researching
- Hates phone tag with reception
- Uncomfortable with pushy sales tactics

**How HeyJarvis helps:**
- SMS communication she can respond to on her time
- Clear information without pressure
- Easy way to schedule without calling
- Respects her time and preferences

**Quotes:**
- "Just text me — I'll respond when I can."
- "I don't want 5 messages a day about one thing."
- "If I'm interested, make it easy for me."

---

### P.3.2 Robert — Elderly Patient

**Demographics:**
- Age: 72
- Retired, fixed income
- Medicare + supplemental
- Communication: Prefers phone calls, occasionally uses email
- Situation: Overdue for hygiene recall, anxious about dental visits

**Goals:**
- Stay healthy
- Affordable care
- Not be pressured into expensive treatments
- Patient, clear communication

**Pain Points:**
- Overwhelmed by technology
- Anxious about dental procedures
- Concerned about costs
- Hard to reach by modern methods
- Confused by insurance

**How HeyJarvis helps:**
- Warm, clear, patient communication
- Gentle reminders that don't pressure
- Human escalation when needed
- Clear explanations of costs and insurance
- Respectful tone appropriate for age

**Quotes:**
- "I wish they'd just call me like a person."
- "I don't understand all these text things."
- "Don't rush me — I need to think about it."

---

### P.3.3 Aisha — Frustrated Former Patient

**Demographics:**
- Age: 28
- Communication: SMS, social media
- Situation: Cancelled appointment due to long wait time, hasn't returned
- Feelings: Annoyed, but not angry

**Goals:**
- Wants good dental care
- Expects respect for her time
- Wants to feel valued

**Pain Points:**
- Had to wait 45 minutes past appointment time last visit
- Staff didn't apologize adequately
- Considering switching practices
- Gets too many marketing messages

**How HeyJarvis helps:**
- Personalized outreach acknowledging her experience
- Appropriate compensation or priority scheduling offer
- Not over-messaging
- Clear, respectful communication

**Quotes:**
- "They wasted my time last time — I'm not rushing back."
- "A simple apology and a shorter wait would mean a lot."
- "Stop sending me coupons — I want respect."

---

### P.3.4 James — Emergency Patient

**Demographics:**
- Age: 52
- Communication: Phone, urgent SMS
- Situation: Broke a tooth, in pain, wedding this Saturday
- Urgency: High

**Goals:**
- Get seen as soon as possible
- Understand what's covered by insurance
- Get pain relief

**Pain Points:**
- Pain is distracting
- Time-sensitive (wedding Saturday)
- Anxious about cost
- Needs clarity, not vague promises

**How HeyJarvis helps:**
- Immediate response
- Urgency detection → priority handling
- Quick insurance verification
- Direct scheduling without friction
- Safety escalation if needed

**Quotes:**
- "I need help NOW."
- "Can you see me today?"
- "Will my insurance cover this?"

**Role:** Onboards new clinics, monitors satisfaction
**Goals:**
- Smooth clinic onboarding
- High practice satisfaction
- Proactive issue resolution
