# Business Model — Revenue Model, Pricing, and Go-to-Market

## BM.1 Revenue Model

### Primary Revenue Streams

```
STREAM 1: SaaS Subscription
├── Per-clinic monthly fee
├── Tiered by patient volume
├── Tiered by features
└── Annual contracts (20% discount)

STREAM 2: Usage-Based Revenue
├── Per-patient management fee
├── Per-message fee (SMS, email)
├── Per-booking fee (revenue share)
└── AI conversation fee (per conversation)

STREAM 3: Professional Services
├── Implementation & onboarding
├── Custom integration
├── Training
├── Premium support

STREAM 4: Data & Insights (Future)
├── Benchmark reports
├── Industry analytics
└── Aggregated market data (anonymized)
```

### SaaS Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Monthly Price | Patients | Clinics | Features |
|------|--------------|----------|---------|----------|
| **Starter** | $299 | Up to 1,000 | 1 | Rules-first recapture, SMS, basic dashboard |
| **Growth** | $799 | Up to 5,000 | 1-3 | AI personalization, email, analytics |
| **Professional** | $1,999 | Up to 25,000 | 1-10 | ML predictions, multi-channel, A/B testing |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited | White-label, custom integrations, dedicated support |

### Per-Usage Pricing

| Item | Price |
|------|-------|
| SMS message | $0.0075 (pass-through + 20%) |
| Email message | $0.001 (pass-through + 20%) |
| AI conversation | $0.05 per conversation |
| Additional patient | $0.50/month per 1,000 patients |
| Revenue share | 5% of recovered revenue above $5,000/month |

### Revenue Calculation Example

```
Clinic: Growth Tier ($799/month)
Patients: 3,000
Monthly outreach: 500 messages
AI conversations: 100/month
Bookings recovered: 30/month
Average appointment value: $200

Revenue from usage:
- SMS: 500 × $0.0075 = $3.75
- AI: 100 × $0.05 = $5.00
- Revenue share: 30 × $200 × 5% = $300

Total monthly revenue: $799 + $3.75 + $5 + $300 = $1,107.75

Clinic ROI:
- Recovered revenue: $6,000/month
- Cost: $1,107.75/month
- ROI: 5.4x
```

## BM.2 Go-to-Market Strategy

### Target Market

```
PRIMARY: Independent dental practices
├── 1-3 dentists
├── 1-2 locations
├── 5-20 staff
├── Using Open Dental, Eaglesoft, or Dentrix
└── Annual revenue: $500K - $3M

SECONDARY: Dental service organizations (DSOs)
├── Multi-location
├── 10+ clinics
├── Centralized management
└── Need multi-tenant platform

TERTIARY: Specialty practices
├── Orthodontics
├── Oral surgery
├── Periodontics
├── Endodontics
└── Higher average appointment value
```

### Market Size

```
TOTAL ADDRESSABLE MARKET (TAM)
├── US dental practices: ~200,000
├── Average practice revenue: ~$1M/year
├── Total market: ~$200B/year
├── Recapture opportunity: 20-30% of revenue
└── TAM for recapture: ~$40-60B/year

SERVICEABLE ADDRESSABLE MARKET (SAM)
├── Practices using supported PMS: ~100,000
├── Digital-ready practices: ~50,000
└── SAM: ~$10-15B/year

SERVICEABLE OBTAINABLE MARKET (SOM) - Year 3
├── Target: 500 practices
├── Average revenue per practice: $1,500/month
└── Annual revenue: $9M
```

### Customer Acquisition Strategy

```
PHASE 1: Direct Sales (Months 1-6)
├── Target: 10 pilot clinics
├── Method: Direct outreach to practice owners
├── Leverage: Industry connections, dental conferences
└── Goal: Case studies and testimonials

PHASE 2: Channel Partnerships (Months 6-18)
├── Partner with PMS vendors (Open Dental, etc.)
├── Partner with dental suppliers
├── Partner with dental marketing agencies
├── Partner with dental consultants
└── Goal: 100 clinics

PHASE 3: Digital Marketing (Months 12-24)
├── SEO for dental practice management keywords
├── Content marketing (blog, webinars)
├── Paid ads (Google, Facebook)
├── Dental review sites
└── Goal: 300 clinics

PHASE 4: Inside Sales (Months 18-36)
├── Inside sales team
├── Self-service signup
├── Freemium tier
├── Referral program
└── Goal: 500+ clinics
```

